The Brigadier General (Signado) Ludovico Arejola On March 25, 1901, General Arejola announced to all inhabitants of Both Camarines and Catanduanes the following: "My dear countrymen and compatriots: No one of you can ignore that I, by launching myself into the field, did not propose to me any other purpose than the independence of our beloved homeland in union of our brothers, because this basual and make happy to the Philippines and the Fillines, was to have self-government. But having seen the solemn declaration and published that the President of the Commission Mr. Taft made in the name of the US Government, corroborated and affirmed by General MacArthur, that America did it to the Philippines not equal the duties of the Filipinos, but to give them liberties and to make it happy.
